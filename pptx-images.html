Toolbox

Pull media out of any slide deck.

Drop a PowerPoint file and download its images as PNGs, plus any video and audio, bundled in one ZIP. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

    Each file is saved once, even if it appears on several slides. PNGs are copied untouched; JPG, GIF, BMP, WebP and SVG are converted to PNG (GIFs keep their first frame). Formats a browser can’t draw, such as EMF, WMF and TIFF, are kept as-is in an “originals” folder. Video and audio are copied byte for byte into “video” and “audio” folders, with no re-encoding and no quality loss. Media that is only linked (a YouTube clip, or a file stored outside the deck) isn’t inside the file, so it can’t be extracted.
